## Evidence review

The assigned editors directly read the local English source segments and checked their questions/cards against them. The original PDF is absent, so fidelity to that PDF has not been independently verified.

- `ABS-001`, `OPENING-001`: mental mechanics and crystallized abilities, cultural expectations, opportunity constraints, and the Dumbledore hypothesis as a proposition to investigate.
- `ATTENTION-001`, `ATTENTION-002`: attentional versus activity engagement, the three SRLP representation levels, negative feedback regulation, allocation policy, the direction of accuracy-goal responsiveness, and capacity versus memory self-efficacy.
- `ACTIVITY-001`: selection and common-cause explanations, cognitive reserve, the randomized ACTIVE design, ten sessions, retention up to five years, and ability-specific transfer. No demonstrated transfer to everyday analogues is distinguished from reasoning trainees' reports of easier instrumental daily activities.
- `ACTIVITY-002`: the broad-lifestyle/narrow-training puzzle, self-direction and novel problems, Senior Odyssey features, wait-list comparison, improved speed, marginal improvement in divergent thinking, and changes in mindfulness and need for cognition. Broad cognitive improvement remains unestablished.
- `CONCLUSION-001`: interactions between the two forms of engagement and the psychosocial mechanisms and conditions that require empirical tests.

Short answers include `crystallized`/`crystallised` variants, `negative feedback loop`/`negative-feedback loop`, and appropriate units or abbreviations. This avoids the earlier mismatch between Korean translation terminology and accepted Korean answers by using the source's English concepts consistently.
